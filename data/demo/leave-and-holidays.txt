Company: Northwind People Ops (fictional).
Title: Leave and Holidays Policy
Revision: 1
Effective date: 2025-04-01

1. Purpose
This policy explains paid time away from work for employees of the fictional company Northwind People Ops.

2. Annual leave
A full-time employee receives 25 days of annual leave each calendar year. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata amount of annual leave. Annual leave does not include public holidays.

3. Public holidays
Northwind observes eight synthetic public holidays: Founders Day, River Festival, Midsummer Break, Harvest Friday, Lantern Day, First Snow, Year Turn, and Atlas Day. These names are fictional.

4. Sick leave
An employee may take up to 10 days of paid sick leave each calendar year. A medical note is requested only after three consecutive sick days.

5. Carryover
Up to 5 unused annual leave days may carry into the next calendar year. Carried days expire on 31 March.
