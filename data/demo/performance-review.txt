1. Purpose
This policy describes the fictional review cycle. A rating is not a disciplinary action and not an employment decision by this demo.

2. Cycle
Formal performance reviews happen twice each year, in May and November.

3. Ratings
The rating scale is Exceeds, Meets, and Developing. Managers write the rating in the synthetic review form.

4. Goals
Each employee agrees three written goals with their manager at the start of the cycle.
